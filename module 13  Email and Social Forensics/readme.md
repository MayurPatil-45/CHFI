
CHFI Module 13: Email and Social Media Forensics
Overview

Email and Social Media Forensics focuses on the identification, collection, preservation, analysis, and reporting of digital evidence from email systems and social networking platforms. This module is critical for investigating cybercrimes such as phishing, email spoofing, identity theft, cyberstalking, fraud, and insider threats.
